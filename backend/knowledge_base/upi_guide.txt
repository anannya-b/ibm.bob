UPI (Unified Payments Interface) – A Complete Guide for Indian Users
======================================================================

WHAT IS UPI?
------------
UPI, or Unified Payments Interface, is a real-time payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) and regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). It allows instant money transfers between bank accounts through a mobile device, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including bank holidays.

Launched in April 2016, UPI has become India's most popular digital payment method, processing billions of transactions every month. It eliminates the need to remember lengthy bank account numbers and IFSC codes — instead, you use a simple Virtual Payment Address (VPA), also called a UPI ID.

HOW UPI WORKS
-------------
UPI operates on a two-factor authentication model:
1. Your mobile number linked to the bank account
2. Your UPI PIN (set during registration)

When you send money:
- You enter the recipient's UPI ID (e.g., name@okaxis or 9876543210@ybl)
- Enter the amount
- Authenticate with your UPI PIN
- The money is debited from your account and credited to the recipient instantly

SETTING UP UPI
--------------
Step 1: Download a UPI-enabled app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, etc.)
Step 2: Register using your mobile number linked to your bank account
Step 3: Add your bank account by selecting your bank from the list
Step 4: The app will auto-detect your account via SMS verification
Step 5: Set a 4 or 6-digit UPI PIN (different from your ATM PIN)
Step 6: Your UPI ID is now created and ready to use

POPULAR UPI APPS IN INDIA
--------------------------
1. **Google Pay (GPay)**: Backed by Google, offers cashback and rewards. UPI IDs end with @okaxis, @okicici, or @oksbi.
2. **PhonePe**: One of the most widely used apps. UPI IDs end with @ybl.
3. **Paytm**: Also offers wallet services alongside UPI. UPI IDs end with @paytm.
4. **BHIM UPI**: Government app by NPCI. Simple and secure for basic transfers.
5. **Amazon Pay**: Integrated with Amazon India for seamless shopping payments.
6. **WhatsApp Pay**: Send money directly within WhatsApp chats.
7. **iMobile (ICICI)**, **YONO (SBI)**, **Kotak Pay** – Bank-specific UPI apps.

TRANSACTION LIMITS
------------------
- Standard UPI limit: ₹1,00,000 per transaction
- Daily limit: ₹1,00,000 (may vary by bank)
- UPI 123PAY (for feature phones): ₹5,000 per transaction
- UPI Lite (for small offline payments): ₹500 per transaction, wallet limit ₹2,000
- Some banks allow higher limits (up to ₹5,00,000) for specific categories like capital markets, insurance, medical, and education

TYPES OF UPI TRANSACTIONS
--------------------------
1. **Send Money (Push)**: You initiate a transfer to someone else
2. **Request Money (Pull/Collect)**: You send a collect request; the payer must approve it
3. **Scan & Pay**: Scan a merchant's QR code to pay
4. **Pay to Contact**: Pay using the recipient's phone number
5. **Recurring Mandates**: Set up auto-pay for subscriptions, EMIs, utility bills

UPI SAFETY TIPS
---------------
🔒 NEVER share your UPI PIN with anyone — not even bank officials or app customer care
🔒 A PIN is required ONLY when sending money, NEVER for receiving money
🔒 If someone sends you money unexpectedly and asks you to "approve" it by entering your PIN, that is a SCAM
🔒 Do not scan QR codes sent by strangers — QR codes for receiving money do NOT exist in legitimate UPI transactions
🔒 Verify the recipient's name after entering their UPI ID before confirming
🔒 Use only official app stores (Google Play / Apple App Store) to download UPI apps
🔒 Enable screen lock and biometric authentication on your phone
🔒 Do not use UPI on public Wi-Fi networks
🔒 Regularly check your transaction history for unauthorized payments
🔒 Link only your primary savings account; avoid linking accounts with large balances

COMMON UPI MISTAKES TO AVOID
-----------------------------
- Entering the wrong UPI ID (always double-check before sending)
- Falling for fake customer care numbers found on Google — always use official app helplines
- Responding to unsolicited collect requests from unknown numbers
- Sharing your screen with strangers during "help" calls

HOW TO REPORT A UPI FRAUD
--------------------------
1. Immediately call your bank's 24x7 helpline to block the account
2. File a complaint on the National Cyber Crime Portal: cybercrime.gov.in
3. Call the Cyber Crime helpline: 1930
4. Lodge an FIR at your local police station
5. Report the fraudulent UPI ID to NPCI via the BHIM app or their website

UPI FOR MERCHANTS
-----------------
Merchants can accept UPI payments through:
- Static QR codes (printed)
- Dynamic QR codes (generated per transaction)
- UPI payment links
- UPI AutoPay for subscriptions

The GST collected on UPI merchant transactions is handled automatically. Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) for UPI transactions is currently zero as per government policy.

FUTURE OF UPI
-------------
- UPI One World: For international visitors to India
- UPI Circle: Delegate payment permissions to family members
- Credit Line on UPI: Link credit products directly to UPI
- Cross-border UPI: Now available in Singapore, UAE, UK, France, Nepal, Bhutan, and more

UPI has transformed India's digital economy and is a model for real-time payment systems worldwide. As of 2024, UPI processes over 10 billion transactions per month, making it the world's largest real-time payment platform.
